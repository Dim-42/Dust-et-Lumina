## Development-only area

This is a deterministic, non-canonical test surface for the current site. It is available in normal local Jekyll builds and deliberately excluded from production artifacts.

- [Foundations]({{ '/dev/reference/foundations/' | relative_url }}) — typography, Markdown, colors, links, lists, tables, and media.
- [Component library]({{ '/dev/reference/components/' | relative_url }}) — real current-site includes, layouts, and repeated markup.
- [Page fixtures]({{ '/dev/reference/pages/' | relative_url }}) — representative compositions of the present architecture.
- [Stress tests]({{ '/dev/reference/stress/' | relative_url }}) — intentionally difficult content and layout states.
- [Legacy markup]({{ '/dev/reference/legacy/' | relative_url }}) — content-facing HTML contracts already used by lore.
